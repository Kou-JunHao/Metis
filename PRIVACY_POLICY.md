# Metis Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Effective Date / 生效日期**: August 12, 2026

## 🌐 English

Welcome to **Metis**, a highly customizable, privacy-first start page extension for your browser. We take your privacy very seriously. 

### 1. No Data Collection
**Metis does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information or usage data.**
- All your settings, preferences, custom search engines, and shortcut links are stored **locally** on your device using your browser's built-in `localStorage` or `IndexedDB`.
- Metis does not use Google Analytics, telemetry, or any other tracking software to monitor your behavior.

### 2. External Services
Metis may connect to external services solely based on your configuration:
- **Search Engines**: When you use the search bar, your query is sent directly to the search engine you selected (e.g., Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo). We do not intercept or log your searches.
- **Dynamic Wallpapers**: If you choose "Bing", "Wikimedia", or "Picsum" as your background source, the extension will fetch the latest image directly from their respective public APIs.

### 3. Permissions
Metis only requests the minimal permissions required to function as your new tab page. We do not require broad host permissions or access to your browsing history.

### 4. Changes to This Policy
Since Metis is fully open-source and local, our data practices will not change silently. Any updates to this policy will be clearly communicated via our GitHub repository.

## 🇨🇳 简体中文

欢迎使用 **Metis**，这是一款高颜值、高定制性且完全注重隐私的浏览器起始页扩展。我们对您的隐私保护极其重视。

### 1. 绝不收集数据
**Metis 不会收集、传输或出售任何个人信息及使用数据。**
- 您配置的所有设置项、自定义搜索引擎、快捷链接、待办事项等数据，均**完全保存在您本地设备**的浏览器存储中（`localStorage` 或 `IndexedDB`）。
- Metis 没有任何遥测（Telemetry）、用户行为分析或数据跟踪代码。

### 2. 必要的外部网络请求
Metis 的运行完全取决于您的个人配置，仅在以下情况会向外发起请求：
- **搜索引擎**：当您在主页进行搜索时，您的搜索词将直接发送给您当前选中的搜索引擎（如 Google、Bing 等）。我们绝不会中转或记录您的搜索历史。
- **每日壁纸**：当您在设置中将壁纸图源选择为“Bing”、“Wikimedia”或“Picsum”时，扩展会直接从对应的公开图片接口获取当天的图像。

### 3. 极简的权限要求
Metis 仅申请作为“新标签页”运作所必需的最少权限。我们不需要读取您的网页内容，也绝不索要您的浏览历史记录。

### 4. 隐私政策的更新
由于 Metis 是完全开源并在本地运行的项目，我们的数据处理方式不会在暗中改变。如果此政策有任何重大变更，我们会在 GitHub 代码仓库中明确公示。
